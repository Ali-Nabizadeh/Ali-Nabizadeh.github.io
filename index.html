Profile

I'm a civil engineer who moved from the construction site into the numbers behind it. After years planning schedules, estimating costs, and supervising quality on industrial and infrastructure projects, I shifted toward project controlling: tracking budgets, running variance analysis, and building the reports that keep stakeholders informed.

Alongside that, I've kept a research thread going. My Master's thesis and two published papers used computer vision to automate site monitoring and construction-waste tracking — work that still shapes how I think about turning site data into decisions.

I hold a B2 German certificate and I'm now based in Augsburg, Germany, where I'm looking for a role in project controlling or commercial project management, ideally on industrial or infrastructure projects. I'm happy to relocate anywhere in Germany for a position that's the right fit.
